# The trail

Salomón asks whether a model that answers closed questions with probabilities can say something worth having about a whole book: how well it is written, how good a read it is, and what kind of book it is. These documents are how it got to the answer it has, in the order things were done. Each stands alone; together they are the argument.

1. [What is known of what makes a book good](books/what-is-known.md). What critics, the craft, the juries of prizes and those who measure readers have held or found, where they agree and where they do not, and what of it the questions were made from. Read before anything was asked of Jev.
2. [What Jev can tell of a chapter](books/jev-experiments.md). What it reads well, where it stops telling things apart and what moves its answers besides the text, tried where the answers were known.
3. [Who takes the notes](books/notes-experiments.md). Jev never sees a whole book, so a language model reads ahead of it: ten were tried for the job.
4. [The books the questions are set on](books/calibration.md). A first valuation, of twenty-nine questions about the qualities of the writing and a panel of passages to set a book against: set on ten books in ladders of known standing, checked on ten others.
5. [What only the whole book shows](books/whole-book.md). How a book is built, how it ends and whose side it takes, asked of an outline and of each chapter in its place.
6. [Other ways of asking](books/other-ways.md). The twenty books as a bench: other sizes of passage, questions about what a reader would do instead of what the writing is like, a duel with a plain retelling; and the way that did best, checked on twelve books it had not seen. It is the one in use.
7. [From a file to a valuation](books/pipeline.md). What is run on a book today, who does what, and what it costs.
8. [How to read a valuation](books/reading-a-valuation.md). What a merit of 0.79 means and what it does not, what can mislead, and the books judged so far to set a number beside.

What each run was, to the call and the cent, is under [records/](../records/README.md), and the scripts are under [experiments/books/jev/](../experiments/books/jev/): an experiment spends what it spends when it is run.

## Everything that was run

All of it on 2026-09-19 but the choice of who takes the notes, with `jev-1.13.0` and, for the notes and the tellings again, `gemini-3.1-flash-lite` through OpenRouter. Jev: 10,379 calls, $1.92 at list price. The notes of thirty-two books and the trials before them: 2,201 calls, $3.08. The passages told again by a language model, some $0.15, are the one thing paid for that left no record of its own.
